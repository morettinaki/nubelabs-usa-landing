The Automation

Full patient journey — step by step

A step-by-step walkthrough of the automated patient lifecycle we built for this group.

Foundation: CRM migration & service mapping

The clinic was running on an outdated management tool with no API access and no automation layer. We migrated to a modern CRM purpose-built for integrations, then mapped all 314 services — including pricing, duration, required specialist, and downstream upsell paths. This service map became the backbone of every automated decision the system makes.

The automated patient journey — step by step

Step 1 AI Booking Agent

Patient contacts the clinic via WhatsApp

The AI assistant receives the message and converses in natural language — identifying whether the patient is new or existing, routing them to the right professional based on their stated need, checking availability, and completing the booking. It handles rescheduling, cancellations, and general questions without any staff involvement.

Automated: Identifies patient in database → routes to appropriate professional → checks real-time availability → books appointment → syncs to all calendars → sends confirmation

Step 2 Calendar Sync

Automated reminders & calendar management

The system sends multi-step WhatsApp reminders at pre-configured intervals before the appointment. The doctor's calendar is updated in real time, with the slot blocked and appointment details attached. Staff are notified automatically — no manual coordination needed.

Automated: Multi-step reminder sequence via WhatsApp → real-time sync to doctor's calendar → staff notification → slot reservation with full patient details

Step 3 Intake Automation

Patient arrives — digital intake form

The patient completes a secure digital form on a clinic tablet or their own phone. The form captures full medical history, insurance status, AI documentation consent, and any updates since their last visit. All data flows directly into their CRM record before the doctor enters the room.

Automated: Form pre-filled with known patient data → insurance captured and matched → AI consent recorded → medical history updated in CRM

Step 4 AI Transcription

Consultation — audio transcription & auto-documentation

Once the doctor has examined the patient and reached a diagnosis, they activate audio transcription and begin explaining findings directly to the patient. The AI listens, understands clinical language, and automatically populates the record in real time: diagnosis, findings, prescriptions, next treatment, and all mandatory fields. When the explanation ends, the record is complete — without the doctor or clinical assistant typing a single word.

Automated: Doctor activates transcription → medical-grade AI transcribes in real time → auto-fills diagnosis, findings, prescriptions, treatment plan in CRM → next treatment flagged and linked to the 314-service map

Step 5 Quote Engine

Automatic quote generation & doctor approval

Immediately after the consultation, the system cross-references the transcribed treatment plan with the clinic's price database and generates a detailed PDF quote. The doctor receives it instantly via WhatsApp and approves or requests edits directly — the AI agent handles revisions and sends back a final version.

Automated: Transcribed plan → price database lookup → PDF generated → sent to doctor via WhatsApp → approval or edit handled → approved PDF forwarded to reception

Step 6 Revenue Capture

Reception — payment & upsell with full context

As the patient moves to the billing area, the approved PDF quote is already attached to their CRM record — complete with diagnosis summary, recommended treatments, and pricing. The billing manager pulls it up instantly, collects payment for the day's visit, and presents additional treatments naturally, with full clinical context already visible.

Automated: Approved PDF attached to CRM record → billing manager accesses from patient file → payment processed → treatment decision recorded → pipeline and billing KPIs updated automatically

Step 7 Re-booking

New appointment booked — cycle restarts

If the patient agrees to additional treatment, the billing manager selects the next specialist from an availability-optimized calendar view. Pricing is already confirmed from the service map. The appointment is booked on the spot, the specialist's calendar is updated, and the patient leaves with a confirmed next visit.

Every interaction throughout this cycle generates structured data: treatments discussed, treatments sold, revenue per appointment, pipeline stage — updated in real time, visible on the dashboard, and interpreted in the bi-weekly consulting report.

Result: Specialist selected → price pre-confirmed → appointment booked → calendar updated → patient WhatsApp confirmation sent → reminder sequence triggered. Zero administrative drops.

Our Methodology

We spend the first weeks understanding how the clinic actually works — not how they think it works. We interview staff, shadow workflows, and document every step before recommending a single tool.
