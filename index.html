The Automated Journey

See exactly how our infrastructure removes humans from repetitive tasks. Below is a real example of our deployment for a dental clinic group—one of the most complex operational environments with over 300 different services mapped.

Phase 1

AI Booking & WhatsApp Routing

The AI assistant receives the inbound message via WhatsApp. It identifies if the client is new or existing, understands natural language, and routes them to the correct professional based on the stated need.

  • Action: Checks real-time availability across calendars.
  • Action: Books appointment and syncs instantly.
Phase 2

Digital Intake & Pre-Consultation

Automated multi-step reminders fire via WhatsApp. Upon arrival, the client completes a secure digital form on their phone or tablet that pre-fills known data.

  • Action: Captures history and consent forms.
  • Result: Data flows directly into the CRM automatically.
Phase 3

Medical-Grade Audio Transcription

The professional activates audio transcription and explains the findings. The AI listens, understands complex language, and maps it.

  • Action: Real-time transcription auto-fills CRM fields.
  • Result: The team is freed from ALL manual typing.
Phase 4

Instant Quote Engine

Immediately after the consultation, the system cross-references the transcribed treatment plan with the entire mapped price database.

  • Action: Generates a detailed PDF quote instantly.
  • Result: Sent for 1-click approval via WhatsApp.
Phase 5

Revenue Capture & Re-booking

As the client moves to billing, the approved PDF is already on the reception screen. The sale happens naturally with full context provided by the AI.

  • Action: Payment processed and recorded in the CRM.
  • Result: The cycle restarts natively. Zero administrative drops.
