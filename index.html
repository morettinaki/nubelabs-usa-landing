Phase 1

AI Booking & WhatsApp Routing

The AI assistant receives the inbound message via WhatsApp. It identifies new vs. existing clients, understands natural language, and routes them to the correct professional.

  • Action: Checks real-time availability across calendars and books the appointment.
Phase 2

Digital Intake & Pre-Consultation

Automated multi-step reminders fire via WhatsApp. Upon arrival, the client completes a secure digital form on their phone or tablet that pre-fills known data.

  • Action: Captures history and consent forms, syncing data directly into the CRM.
Phase 3

Medical-Grade Audio Transcription

The professional activates audio transcription and explains the findings. The AI listens, understands complex clinical language, and maps it to your service database.

  • Action: Real-time transcription auto-fills CRM fields.
  • Result: The team is freed from ALL manual typing.
Phase 4

Instant Quote Engine

Immediately after the consultation, the system cross-references the transcribed treatment plan with the entire mapped price database.

  • Action: Generates a detailed PDF quote instantly.
  • Result: Sent for 1-click approval via WhatsApp.

Revenue Capture & Re-booking

As the client moves to billing, the approved PDF is already on the reception screen. The sale happens naturally with full context provided by the AI.

  • Action: Payment processed and recorded in the CRM.

What exactly does Nubelabs do?

Nubelabs automates the entire patient journey for multi-location clinics: from the first WhatsApp message to re-booking after billing. AI scheduling, automated intake, medical-grade transcription, and instant quote generation — all without manual staff involvement.

Who qualifies to work with Nubelabs?

Multi-location healthcare or wellness businesses with at least 2 locations and $1M+ in annual revenue. Our 1% Rule requires that our total cost represents less than 1% of your annual revenue.

How many hours per week will my practice save?

Our dental group case study recovered 21+ hours per week per location across scheduling, intake, clinical documentation, quoting, and billing. Your specific result depends on your current process maturity and location count.

How much does Nubelabs cost?

Pricing is custom-built by location count and service volume. A practice doing $2M/year would need our total 12-month cost to stay under $20,000. If the math doesn't work for you under our 1% Rule, we won't take your engagement.

If our pre-launch math doesn't unequivocally prove you will make your investment back through hours saved and errors eliminated, we refuse to take your money. No exceptions.

Does this work for non-dental healthcare clinics?

Yes. Our automation framework applies to any multi-location healthcare, wellness, medical aesthetics, physical therapy, or B2C service business with a structured client journey. The system adapts to your specific service catalog during the mapping phase.

Will automation replace my staff?

No. It eliminates repetitive tasks so your existing team focuses on higher-value work. As you scale to new locations, you simply won't need to add administrative staff proportionally.

What is the difference between Nubelabs and practice management software?

Software gives you a system to store data; staff still enter it manually. Nubelabs actively performs the tasks for you — the AI schedules autonomously, transcribes in real time, generates quotes without input, and closes the billing loop automatically. No manual data entry required at any step.

How does the audio transcription work clinically?

The AI listens to the consultation in real time, understands clinical language, maps identified treatments to your service catalog, and auto-fills CRM fields — while the conversation is still happening. Zero typing.
